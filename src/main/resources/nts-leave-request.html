Leave Policy Guidelines

📅 Annual Leave

  • Must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance
  • Maximum 5 consecutive days without special approval
  • Cannot be carried forward beyond December 31st
  • Requires manager approval

🏥 Sick Leave

  • Can be taken without prior notice
  • Medical certificate required for 3+ days
  • Notify supervisor within 2 hours of start time
  • Unused days carry forward to next year

👤 Personal Days

  • 24 hours advance notice required
  • Subject to operational requirements
  • Cannot be taken during peak periods
  • Lost if not used by year end

🍼 Maternity/Paternity Leave

  • Must provide 4 weeks notice (or ASAP)
  • Medical documentation required
  • Contact HR for detailed arrangements
  • Additional unpaid leave may be available
